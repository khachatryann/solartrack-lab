# BitVolt Lab launch media

28-second, 1080 × 1920 caption-led launch video for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. No music or voice recording is included.

The animated power traces use `results/runs/clouds-step0.6-sample0.25.json` from commit `598802bc3d34ce5f1acfb0c172dba99d336bae45`. Full-run energy-tracking figures are 98.40635758366369% (fixed voltage) and 94.25479716450054% (P&O). Values on screen are rounded to two decimals. Traces are played back faster than simulated time.

The video states that this is an illustrative simulation without hardware measurements. These results do not establish a universal controller ranking or converter efficiency.

AI assisted the project and media preparation. See `docs/AI_ASSISTANCE.md` for the project statement.
